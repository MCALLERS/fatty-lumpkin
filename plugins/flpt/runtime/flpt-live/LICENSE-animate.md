# cth9191/animate · MIT notice

flpt-live derives parts of its runtime from cth9191/animate: in `flpt-live.js`, the mulberry32 + FNV-1a random
numbers, the EZ easings, the closed-form springs, the cameras (`cam`, `push`, `bump`) and the by-angle shape morph;
in `kit/ink.js`, the hand wobble, resampling and pressure ribbon. The Riso demo kit (`demos/kits/riso/`) is a 16:9
port of animate's riso style and carries this notice as its own `LICENSE`. This notice travels with every copy of
these files (the app's `vendor/flpt-live/`, the site's `vendor/flpt-live/`, every rendered scene's `assets/`).

authors: cth9191 (original), Bombadillo AI (flpt-live, 16:9 ports)
